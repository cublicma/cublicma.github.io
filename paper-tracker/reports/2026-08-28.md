**analysis_zh**: 这篇工作主要提出一种腱驱动五指灵巧手并集成分布式触觉感知；它要解决的是灵巧机器人手在灵巧度和触觉感知能力上的双重瓶颈，尤其是要同时兼顾柔顺性、输出力和接触感知。方法上，作者采用软-刚混合结构，并在五根手指的远端和中段指节上布置双模态触觉元件，使系统能够同时检测静态接触与动态力变化。实验包括手势计数、拇指-食指捏取、物体抓取以及瓶体抓取触觉记录，证明了该一体化执行-感知系统的可行性。公开摘要没有给出更细致的定量指标、对照基线或长时稳定性结果，因此目前更适合把它视作一项面向灵巧手本体设计与多点触觉集成的硬件型贡献，而不是已完成系统级性能闭环验证的成熟方案。  
**analysis_en**: The main contribution is a tendon-driven five-fingered hand with distributed tactile perception. It addresses a core bottleneck in dexterous robotics: a hand must provide both sufficient dexterity and tactile sensing if it is to support complex manipulation, yet those two requirements are often hard to balance. The method uses a soft-rigid-hybrid structure to combine compliance with operational force, and places dual-modality tactile elements on the distal and middle phalanges of all five fingers so the system can detect both static contact and dynamic force variation. The experiments reported in the abstract include counting gestures, finger-to-thumb pinching, object grasping, and bottle-grasp tactile recording, which demonstrate the feasibility of the integrated actuation-perception system. The public summary does not provide detailed quantitative metrics, comparison baselines, or long-horizon stability data, so the current evidence supports treating it as a hardware-centric contribution to dexterous-hand design and tactile integration rather than a fully validated closed-loop manipulation system.
