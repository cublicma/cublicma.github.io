## 条目

### 1. Physics-Informed Sliding-Window Particle Filtering for Tactile-Only In-Hand 6-DoF Object Pose Refinement
- 首次公开日期：2026-08-18
- 作者/机构：Lingjun Shao, Ying Zhang, Xiangfei Li, Xiangyang Li, Huan Zhao, Zhenyu Wang, Han Ding
- 类型：paper
- 相关性：该工作直接面向灵巧手内的物体位姿精修，触觉是核心信号，属于典型的灵巧抓取和在手操作问题。
- 新颖性信号：arXiv 新公开，并明确给出 physics-informed、sliding-window、particle filtering 与 tactile-only 的完整技术路线。
- 中文分析：该工作主要提出一种面向触觉仅输入的手内 6 自由度物体位姿精修方法。它要解决的是抓持后视觉容易失效、而手内微调又高度依赖接触信息的问题。方法上，作者在 SE(3) 上构建 physics-informed particle filter，用密集全手触觉测量更新位姿信念，并把 active-contact 几何一致性、法向力一致性、摩擦锥可行性、零力负证据与可选可行性约束合入似然；同时用 sliding-window 融合近期触觉帧缓解单帧歧义，并用 potential-field 引导 proposal 避免手-物体穿透，最后通过对称感知重采样保留多个可能模态。实验在 Allegro Hand V5 和 5 个物体上进行，结果显示该方法的 normalized ADD-S 低于触觉几何法、常规粒子滤波和学习基线，消融也验证了时间融合、势场引导和多模态保留的作用。总结而言，这项工作把触觉驱动的位姿修正做得更物理一致、更稳健，但公开信息对更大规模任务覆盖、失败条件和与更强视觉/多模态系统的完整对比仍不充分。
- English analysis: This paper presents a physics-informed sliding-window particle filter for tactile-only in-hand 6-DoF object pose refinement. Its main contribution is a contact-centric pose-belief maintenance method that uses dense whole-hand tactile measurements to refine the pose of grasped objects when vision is unavailable or heavily occluded. The problem is important because in-hand adjustment after grasp closure is dominated by ambiguous contact information, sparse excitation, and object symmetries. The method runs a particle filter on SE(3) and builds the likelihood from active-contact signed-distance consistency, force-normal alignment, friction-cone feasibility, zero-force negative evidence, and optional feasibility guards; it also uses a sliding window over recent tactile frames to reduce single-frame ambiguity, a potential-field-guided proposal to avoid hand-object penetration, and symmetry-aware resampling to preserve multiple plausible modes. Experiments on an Allegro Hand V5 with five objects show lower normalized ADD-S than tactile-only geometric, particle-filter, and learning baselines, and ablations support the contributions of temporal fusion, potential guidance, and mode preservation. Overall, the work is a strong tactile localization contribution for dexterous manipulation, but the public description is still limited on task scale, failure cases, and broader comparisons against stronger vision or multimodal systems.
