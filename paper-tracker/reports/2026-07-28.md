## English Summary

## 重点条目

### 1. N0-VTLA: Scaling Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Model with Latent Tactile Tokens
**日期**：2026-07-25  
**作者/机构**：NeoteAI Team & Fudan TEAI Team  
**类型**：paper  
**相关性**：这项工作把视觉、触觉、语言和动作统一到同一模型，并直接报告了灵巧手真实机器人任务结果，属于触觉驱动灵巧操作的核心方向。  
**新颖性信号**：宣称是首个在大规模触觉数据上预训练的 VTLA 模型，并通过分阶段触觉路径集成与离线策略改进提升接触丰富操作能力。  
**原始链接**：arXiv、官方项目页、代码仓库。  
**置信度**：0.96

**分析（中文）**：这篇工作的主要工作是提出 `N0-VTLA`，把视觉-触觉-语言-动作统一进一个大模型，并用潜在触觉 token 和分阶段触觉路径集成来学习接触丰富操作；它要解决的是现有 VLA/操作模型普遍忽视触觉、在接触阶段容易失稳的问题。方法上，作者先用大规模视触觉机器人数据做预训练，再加入可预测触觉分支，并结合 advantage-conditioned offline policy improvement 继续优化下游策略。实验覆盖真实机器人和仿真接触丰富基准，公开结果显示它在 9 个真实 NeoReal 任务上全部优于基线，在 20 个仿真任务上平均成功率达到 63.8%，明显高于最强基线 44.0%；在三项长时程真实任务上也达到 75% 至 95% 的成功率。整体结论是触觉原生化能显著提升精细操作与轨迹平滑性，但公开材料对失败模式、更多手型与更广泛物体分布的泛化边界说明仍有限。  

**Analysis (English)**: The main contribution is `N0-VTLA`, a unified vision-tactile-language-action model built around latent tactile tokens and staged tactile-path integration for contact-rich manipulation. It addresses a common weakness in current VLA-style systems: they often underuse touch and become unstable during contact. The method first pretrains on large-scale visuo-tactile robot data, then adds a predictive tactile pathway, and finally applies advantage-conditioned offline policy improvement for downstream adaptation. Experiments span real robots and simulation, and the reported results are strong: the model wins all nine real-robot NeoReal tasks, reaches 63.8% mean success on a 20-task simulation suite versus 44.0% for the strongest baseline, and achieves 75% to 95% success on three long-horizon real-robot tasks. The overall conclusion is that making touch a first-class modality materially improves fine manipulation and trajectory quality, but the public information is still limited on failure cases, cross-hand generalization, and the full breadth of object distributions.

### 2. N0-TWAM: Scaling Tactile-Native World Action Model for Contact-Rich Manipulation
**日期**：2026-07-25  
**作者/机构**：NeoteAI Team & Fudan TEAI Team  
**类型**：paper  
**相关性**：这项工作把触觉作为原生模态用于接触丰富操作，并在真实与仿真基准上验证，对灵巧操作学习具有直接价值。  
**新颖性信号**：宣称是首个大规模训练的 tactile-native world-action model，覆盖 6 种具身形态和 450 个任务。  
**原始链接**：arXiv、官方项目页、代码仓库。  
**置信度**：0.95

**分析（中文）**：这篇工作的主要工作是提出 `N0-TWAM`，把触觉从“附加输入”提升为世界-动作模型的原生模态，让模型同时预测未来视觉、未来触觉和动作；它要解决的是接触丰富操作中仅依赖视觉容易在接触前后阶段失去精细控制的问题。方法上，作者用大规模视触觉联合训练预训练一个 asymmetric Mixture-of-Transformers 架构，并让视频、触觉和动作分别通过专门专家建模，再在下游任务上进行精细化部署。实验覆盖六种具身形态、450 个任务，以及真实与仿真接触丰富基准，公开结果显示它在真实机器人八项任务上平均成功率达到 46.3%，高于视觉语言动作基线 `pi_0.5` 的 30.0%，在 NeoSim 上也以 49.4 的平均成绩领先最强基线 45.8。整体来看，这证明了把触觉内化到世界模型中能提升精细操作能力，但公开信息对硬件差异、传感器噪声和更复杂长时程任务的鲁棒性仍未完全展开。  

**Analysis (English)**: The main contribution is `N0-TWAM`, a tactile-native world-action model that predicts future vision, future touch, and action as a single coupled problem. It addresses the core limitation of vision-only or tactile-as-side-input pipelines in contact-rich manipulation, where fine control often degrades around contact transitions. The method pretrains an asymmetric Mixture-of-Transformers on large-scale visuo-tactile data, with separate experts for video, tactile, and action modeling, and then deploys the model on downstream manipulation tasks. Experiments span six embodiments, 450 tasks, and both real and simulated contact-rich benchmarks. The reported results show a 46.3% macro-average success rate on eight real-robot tasks, ahead of the 30.0% from the `pi_0.5` baseline, and a 49.4 average on NeoSim, also ahead of the strongest baseline at 45.8. The overall conclusion is that making touch native to the world model improves precise manipulation, but the public material still leaves open questions about hardware variation, sensor noise, and robustness on even longer-horizon tasks.
