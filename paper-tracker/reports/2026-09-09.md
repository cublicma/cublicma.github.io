## 主要条目

### 1. Dex-X: Learning Visual-Tactile Dexterous Manipulation From Human Videos with Simulated Interaction

**中文**

Dex-X 的主要工作是把人类视频转化为可部署的视触觉灵巧操作策略，解决的是灵巧操作中高质量示教稀缺、视频里缺少触觉信息、而真实机器人侧采集成本又很高的问题。其方法核心是把仿真当作“触觉补全引擎”：先从单目人类演示重建手物交互，再在物理一致的仿真接触动力学中生成原视频没有的触觉监督，随后训练视触觉策略，并蒸馏为可在点云和触觉输入上运行的部署策略。公开结果显示，它在多类抓取和接触丰富工具使用任务上实现了灵巧手臂平台的零样本 sim-to-real 迁移，教师策略在仿真中六类任务平均成功率为 65.9%，蒸馏后的策略在真实世界立方体抓取上达到 93%，在更难的桌面清理任务上达到 53%，并对未见几何体表现出一定泛化。总体上，这项工作把互联网视频与可执行灵巧控制连接起来，路线清晰且很有扩展性；限制在于当前公开信息主要来自摘要，关于重建误差、失败模式、消融设置和更广泛手型上的稳健性仍不充分。

**English**

Dex-X’s main contribution is a pipeline that turns human videos into deployable visual-tactile dexterous manipulation policies, addressing the lack of high-quality demonstrations, the absence of tactile signals in videos, and the high cost of collecting robot-side data. The key method is to use simulation as a “tactile completion engine”: the system reconstructs hand-object interaction from monocular human demonstrations, then leverages physically grounded contact dynamics in simulation to synthesize tactile supervision that is missing from the original videos. It then trains visual-tactile policies and distills them into deployable policies that run on point-cloud and tactile inputs. The public results report zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on a dexterous hand-arm platform across grasping and contact-rich tool-use tasks, with the teacher policy reaching 65.9% average success over six task categories in simulation, the distilled policy achieving 93% on real-world cube picking and 53% on the harder table-cleaning task, and some generalization to unseen object geometries. Overall, the work provides a clear and scalable bridge from internet video to executable dexterous control. The main limitation is that the public information is still mostly abstract-level, so reconstruction error, failure modes, ablation details, and robustness across broader hand embodiments remain under-specified.
