## 1. NestDex: Nested Policy Learning with Copilot-Assisted Teleoperation for Dexterous Manipulation

这项工作的主要工作是提出 NestDex，一种面向灵巧操作的嵌套策略框架，把低层的手部技能学习和高层的视觉语言技能选择结合起来。它解决的问题是：灵巧任务往往既需要稳定、可复用的手内操作能力，又需要根据场景和指令在不同技能之间切换，而单一端到端策略难以兼顾两者。方法上，作者利用 copilot-assisted teleoperation 采集灵巧操作数据，学习可复用的内层 hand skill，并用视觉语言模块在外层选择合适技能，实现分层执行。公开材料显示，作者在真实灵巧操作场景中进行验证，并展示该设计能更好地把复杂任务拆解为可执行子技能。结果表明，这种嵌套式组织方式有助于提升任务执行的可控性与泛化性；不过，公开信息对具体基准、对照方法、硬件配置和定量结果披露仍不充分，因此目前更适合把它视为一个有潜力的系统框架，而不是已完全定论的最终方案。

The main contribution of this work is NestDex, a nested policy framework for dexterous manipulation that combines low-level hand-skill learning with high-level vision-language skill selection. It addresses the practical problem that dexterous tasks require both stable, reusable in-hand abilities and context-dependent switching among skills, while a single end-to-end policy often struggles to balance the two. Methodologically, the authors collect teleoperation data with copilot assistance, learn reusable inner hand skills, and use a vision-language module to choose the appropriate skill at the outer level, enabling hierarchical execution. The public materials indicate validation in real dexterous manipulation settings and suggest that the design helps decompose complex tasks into executable subskills. The reported results imply that this nested organization improves controllability and generalization, but the public information is still insufficient on the exact benchmark suite, baselines, hardware setup, and quantitative metrics, so it is best viewed as a promising system framework rather than a fully settled final answer.
