### 1. Fiber Optic Sensing Glove for High Performance Dexterous Manipulation Capture

**类型**: paper  
**首次公开日期**: 2026-08-25  
**作者或机构**: J.D. Peiffer, Taylor Niehues, Li Guan, Ziyi Kou, Ergys Ristani  
**相关性**: 该工作直接面向灵巧操作过程中的手部姿态捕捉，核心目标是解决手部遮挡、光照不稳定和传感器漂移导致的高质量示教获取问题，和灵巧手数据采集、遥操作及模仿学习高度相关。  
**新颖性信号**: 这是 2026-08-25 新提交的 arXiv 论文，摘要中明确给出 2 小时的灵巧物体操作数据集、60 Hz 重建管线以及与 motion capture 的定量对比，属于新增且可验证的公开条目。  
**链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24572  
**置信度**: 0.97  
**analysis_zh**: 这篇工作主要提出一种基于光纤传感的手套，用于高性能灵巧操作捕捉；它要解决的是灵巧操作中手部位姿难以稳定获取的问题，尤其是视觉方法易受遮挡和光照影响、现有传感手套又容易漂移和受磁干扰。方法上，作者用多芯形状传感光纤重建每根纤维的三维形状，并通过统一参考系配准与新的逆运动学求解器，在 60 Hz 下恢复完整手部姿态。实验包括 5 名受试者的 2 小时灵巧物体操作数据集，并与 motion capture 真实值对比；结果显示平均指尖位置误差为 7.2 mm，经一次工厂标定后可降到 4.9 mm。总体来看，它为高保真示教采集和双手虚拟遥操作提供了实用方案，但公开信息尚不足以判断其在更复杂动作、长期佩戴舒适性和跨手型泛化上的极限。  
**analysis_en**: The main contribution is a fiber-optic sensing glove for high-performance capture of dexterous manipulation. It addresses a practical problem in hand-centric robotics: reliable hand-pose tracking during dexterous tasks is difficult because vision suffers from occlusion and lighting changes, while many sensorized gloves drift or are affected by magnetic interference. The method reconstructs each multi-core shape-sensing fiber in 3D, registers the fibers in a shared hand frame, and uses a new inverse-kinematics solver to recover full hand pose at 60 Hz. The experiments use a 2-hour dexterous object-manipulation dataset from 5 subjects and compare against motion-capture ground truth. Results reported in the abstract show 7.2 mm mean fingertip position error, improving to 4.9 mm after a one-time factory calibration that transfers across users and sessions. Overall, the paper offers a credible path toward high-fidelity demonstration capture and bimanual virtual teleoperation, but the public abstract does not provide enough detail to judge performance on more diverse task families, long-term wearability, or broader cross-user generalization.  
**original source links**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24572

### 2. A Durable Vision-Based Tactile Fingertip for Robotic Manipulation

**类型**: paper  
**首次公开日期**: 2026-08-25  
**作者或机构**: F. Richard Cottrell, Megha H. Tippur, Edward H. Adelson  
**相关性**: 该工作直接针对机器人灵巧操作中的触觉感知核心部件，重点不是通用抓取，而是提高视觉触觉指尖在高磨损、重复受力场景下的耐久性，属于灵巧手触觉传感的关键支撑技术。  
**新颖性信号**: 这是 2026-08-25 新提交的 arXiv 论文，摘要明确给出加速磨损测试与重复加载测试，并与 GelSight Mini 和 DIGIT 做了对比，属于新增且有定量支撑的公开工作。  
**链接**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24242  
**置信度**: 0.96  
**analysis_zh**: 这篇工作主要提出一种耐久型视觉触觉指尖，用于机器人灵巧操作中的接触感知；它要解决的是现有商用视觉触觉传感器在磨损和集中载荷下容易失效的问题，这会限制其在高强度机器人任务中的实用性。方法上，作者采用软硅胶凝胶、带纹理的薄 TPU 保护膜以及可更换感知模块，形成可维护的传感结构，并通过旋转砂磨和 39.2 N 重复按压两类加速测试评估耐久性。实验结果显示，在定义条件下该传感器可承受约 2 到 3 小时砂磨，重复按压测试中 9 个样品都保持可用，且与 GelSight Mini、DIGIT 相比，耐久性提升超过两个数量级。总体结论是，渐进式损伤与快速更换式结构有助于实现更实用的触觉指尖，但公开摘要未说明其在真实灵巧操作闭环控制中的系统级表现，也未给出更复杂接触任务下的长期验证。  
**analysis_en**: The main contribution is a durable vision-based tactile fingertip for robotic manipulation. It tackles a concrete problem in dexterous-hand systems: commercial vision-based tactile sensors often wear out quickly under abrasion and repeated concentrated loads, which limits deployment in demanding manipulation workloads. The proposed method combines a soft silicone gel, a textured thin TPU protective film, and a replaceable sensing cartridge to improve survivability while keeping tactile imaging usable. The experiments use two accelerated durability tests, namely a rotating-drum sanding test and a repetitive probe test at 39.2 N and 45 cycles per minute. According to the abstract, the sensor reached rupture after roughly 2 to 3 hours of sanding, and all nine tested sensors remained functionally usable during repetitive loading, with endpoints at 5, 6, and 8 days. Compared with GelSight Mini and DIGIT specimens, the reported durability improvement is more than two orders of magnitude under the tested conditions. Overall, the paper is a strong hardware contribution for tactile dexterity, but the public information is still insufficient to determine how the design performs in full closed-loop dexterous manipulation tasks, long-horizon field use, or broader system integration.  
**original source links**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24242
