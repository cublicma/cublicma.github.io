## 条目
### 1. UniCross: Unified Cross-Skill Dexterous Manipulation Synthesis
- 首次公开日期：2026-07-30
- 作者/机构：Hui Zhang, Julian Ferchow, Jie Song, Mirko Meboldt
- 类型：paper
- 相关性：这篇工作把灵巧抓取、重定位、内旋转和内平移四类核心灵巧操作统一到一个共享表述里，直接围绕灵巧机器人手的技能组合与长程操作展开，而不是把灵巧手当作附带平台。
- 新颖性信号：arXiv 于 2026-07-30 首次公开，主题明确是跨技能灵巧操作统一建模，并给出项目页，属于本轮新增的核心相关条目。

UniCross 的主要工作是把灵巧抓取、重定位、内旋转和内平移统一到同一套 hand-object relational formulation 中，解决的是现有方法把不同灵巧技能拆成彼此割裂的任务、从而难以连续组合和长程执行的问题。方法上，它让四类技能共享相同的状态空间、动作空间和目标结构，先训练各技能策略，再通过蒸馏得到单一跨技能策略，并保持统一的观测表示与奖励设计。公开信息显示作者在仿真中评估了各技能性能、对未见物体的泛化、抗扰性以及长程技能串联，还测试了不同手型间的迁移。结果表明单一策略能在每个技能上保持较强表现，并支持跨技能无缝切换。总体来看，这项工作把多种灵巧操作视为共享任务空间中的不同实例，思路清晰且对通用灵巧操作有推进作用；但公开摘要和页面对真实机器人实机规模、具体基线数值和失败模式披露仍有限，限制了对落地能力的完整判断。

UniCross’s main contribution is a unified hand-object relational formulation for grasping, relocation, in-hand rotation, and in-hand translation, addressing the fragmentation in prior dexterous-manipulation work where each skill was modeled separately and could not be composed cleanly over long horizons. The method gives all four skills the same state space, action space, and objective structure, then trains per-skill policies before distilling them into a single cross-skill policy with a shared observation representation and reward design. The public material says the authors evaluate per-skill performance, generalization to unseen objects, robustness to disturbances, long-horizon skill chaining, and transfer across hand morphologies in simulation. The reported outcome is that one policy performs strongly across the individual skills while enabling seamless cross-skill transitions. Overall, this is a meaningful step toward general-purpose dexterous manipulation because it treats multiple skills as instances of one shared task space; however, the public abstract and page do not fully disclose the scale of real-robot validation, exact baseline numbers, or failure modes, so the practical limits are still only partially characterized.
