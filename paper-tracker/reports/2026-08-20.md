## 1. ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning

**中文分析：** 这项工作主要提出一个用于灵巧操作的大规模强化学习框架 ADEPT，解决的是多自由度多指机器人在长时序任务中难以从零开始学习、且跨任务迁移时容易丢失已有灵巧技能的问题。方法上，作者先在通用物体复位任务上预训练灵巧策略，再把该策略作为先验用于下游任务后训练，并结合行为克隆蒸馏、critic warm-up 和保守的 on-policy 更新来稳定迁移；同时引入 joint-space Geometric Fabric 作为策略与机器人之间的安全与运动学调节器。实验在 23 DoF Kuka-Allegro 和 29 DoF Flexiv-Sharpa 两种实体平台上进行，配合双 RGB 相机与触觉传感，结果显示系统可以对下游任务的复位阶段实现 zero-shot，且能在挑战性初始状态下完成长时序任务。总体来看，它把“先学通用灵巧技能，再高效迁移到新任务”做成了可工作的路线，但公开摘要对具体基准、失败模式、消融细节和与更广泛方法的完整数值对比仍披露有限。  

**English analysis:** This work introduces ADEPT, a large-scale reinforcement learning framework for dexterous manipulation that addresses a core problem in multi-finger robotics: learning long-horizon behaviors from scratch is hard, and transferring dexterous skills to new tasks often destroys previously acquired capabilities. The main idea is to pretrain a dexterous policy on a generic object-reposing task and then use that policy as a prior for downstream post-training, with behavior-cloning distillation, critic warm-up, and conservative on-policy updates to stabilize transfer. The method also adds a joint-space Geometric Fabric to mediate between the RL policy and the robot’s kinematics and safety constraints. Experiments on two physical embodiments, a 23 DoF Kuka-Allegro and a 29 DoF Flexiv-Sharpa, use dual RGB cameras and vision-based tactile sensors, and the abstract reports zero-shot reposing on downstream tasks plus successful long-horizon execution from difficult initial states. The overall conclusion is that pretrain-then-post-train is a promising path for scalable dexterity, but the public abstract gives limited information about exact benchmarks, failure cases, ablation details, and broader numerical comparisons.  

## 2. LabDex: A Hierarchical Benchmark for Dexterous Manipulation in Laboratories

**中文分析：** 这项工作主要提出 LabDex，一个面向化学实验室灵巧操作的真实世界数据集和层级式基准，解决的是现有基准无法同时覆盖灵巧手使用、真实实验室交互和多阶段实验流程的问题。方法上，作者把任务组织为三层结构：Atomic Skills、Compositional Skills 和 Long-Horizon Laboratory Workflows，并首次把真实平台和仿真平台统一到共同框架下，提供标准化任务定义、示范数据和评测协议。实验对代表性机器人学习方法做了跨层级评测，覆盖真实环境与仿真环境，结果表明该任务设计与示范数据有效，且能支持不同层级灵巧操作任务上的训练与系统评估。总体而言，LabDex 把“实验室里的灵巧手操作”从零散任务提升为可比较、可扩展的基准，但摘要没有给出具体成功率、样本规模、硬件细节或失败案例，因此对上限能力和推广边界的判断仍需后续全文。  

**English analysis:** This paper presents LabDex, a large-scale real-world dataset and benchmark for dexterous manipulation in chemistry laboratories, addressing the gap that existing benchmarks do not jointly capture dexterous hand use, real lab interactions, and multi-stage experimental procedures. The method organizes laboratory work into a three-level hierarchy, namely Atomic Skills, Compositional Skills, and Long-Horizon Laboratory Workflows, and, importantly, unifies real-world and simulation platforms under one framework with standardized task definitions, demonstrations, and evaluation protocols. The experiments perform cross-level evaluations of representative robot learning methods in both real and simulated settings, and the abstract states that the benchmark design and demonstration data are effective and that the suite supports training and systematic evaluation across laboratory dexterous manipulation tasks. The conclusion is that LabDex provides a foundation for autonomous-lab research by making dexterous manipulation in laboratories measurable and compositional, but the public abstract does not disclose concrete success rates, dataset scale, hardware specifics, or failure analysis, so the limits of generalization remain under-specified.
