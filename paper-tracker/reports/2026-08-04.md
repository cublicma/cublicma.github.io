### 1. MANGO-Grasp: Mahalanobis Fields over Geometry-Oriented 3D Gaussians for Cross-Embodiment Dexterous Grasping

- 首次公开时间：2026-08-03
- 作者/机构：Heng Zhang, Kevin Yuchen Ma, Mike Zheng Shou, Weisi Lin, Yan Wu
- 类型：paper
- 相关性：面向异构多指灵巧手的跨具身灵巧抓取，把灵巧抓取与手型迁移作为核心贡献，而不是泛化的抓取或普通机械臂操作。
- Novelty：2026-08-03 首次公开，晚于上一轮成功运行；同时给出真实机器人实验和对未见手型的零样本迁移结果，属于明确新增。
- 原始链接：
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02014
  - https://connor-zh.github.io/MANGO-Grasp/
- 置信度：0.96
- analysis_zh：该工作主要提出 MANGO-Grasp，用几何导向的 3D Gaussian 原语表示物体，并把机器人手表示为带形态与运动学信息的表面关键点描述，以跨具身灵巧抓取为核心问题，解决不同多指手之间缺乏统一交互建模与迁移能力的难点。方法上，它用 Mahalanobis fields 表达关键点与原语之间的接触方向性，在训练时作为交互预测目标，在推理时作为优化引导，并用同一套优化形式与超参数覆盖不同手型。实验上，作者在 CMAP 和 MultiGripperGrasp 基准上报告了相对最强已见手基线最高 8.24 个百分点的仿真提升，对未见的 SharpaWave 手实现零样本迁移，且真实世界实验平均成功率达到 86%。总体来看，这一方法在表示与优化层面都加强了跨手型灵巧抓取，但公开材料里关于代码、数据发布时机、更多消融和失败案例的说明仍不充分。
- analysis_en：This work’s main contribution is MANGO-Grasp, a cross-embodiment dexterous grasping framework that represents objects as geometry-oriented 3D Gaussian primitives and robot hands as morpho-kinematic descriptors derived from surface keypoints. It addresses the problem of transferring stable grasps across heterogeneous multi-fingered hands with little or no embodiment-specific tuning. The method models interaction with Mahalanobis fields over keypoint-primitive pairs, using them both as training targets and as optimization guidance at inference, so the same formulation can be reused across embodiments. In experiments, the authors report up to an 8.24-point improvement over the strongest seen-hand baseline on CMAP and MultiGripperGrasp in simulation, zero-shot transfer to the unseen SharpaWave hand with up to a 16.57-point gain over the strongest zero-shot baseline, and 86% success in real-world trials. The overall conclusion is that the representation and optimization choices meaningfully improve cross-hand dexterous grasping, but the public information is still limited regarding code release timing, data availability, and deeper ablations or failure analysis.
