**English Summary**

This run found 2 newly relevant items: `TacWAM` and `FasTac`. TacWAM brings mechanics-aware tactile prediction into a world-action model and reports strong gains on real contact-rich tasks over visual and prior multimodal baselines; FasTac targets curved fingertips for dexterous manipulation and jointly addresses high-precision 3D reconstruction, three-axis force estimation, and high-speed edge deployment, so it is a substantive sensing-side addition for dexterous hands.

## 1. TacWAM: Anchor-Guided World Action Model with Mechanics-Aware Tactile Prediction

这篇工作把机械感知触觉预测纳入世界-动作模型，直接服务于接触丰富灵巧操作和 in-hand manipulation，不是泛化的视觉操控论文。TacWAM 的主要工作是把机械感知触觉预测引入世界-动作模型，用来解决接触丰富操作里仅靠视觉未来无法刻画力、形变、剪切和滑移的问题。方法上，它用 SAF 触觉编码器把触觉外观、密集力场和形变流映射到共享潜空间，再用触觉历史编码器补足接触阶段的时间上下文，并通过 Anchor-Guided Tri-Modal Attention 将未来触觉监督与动作分支隔离，避免把训练时才可见的信息泄漏到执行阶段。实验在 4 个真实接触丰富任务上进行，覆盖脆弱抓取、持续表面接触和动态 in-hand manipulation。结果显示平均成功率达到 75.0%，比最强基线高 37.5 个百分点，消融也表明去掉触觉历史或放松未来目标约束都会退化。总体来看，它清楚地证明了“未来触觉监督 + 受限信息流”对灵巧操作有效；但公开材料对机器人平台规模、数据量与失败案例披露仍有限。

TacWAM’s main contribution is to bring mechanics-aware tactile prediction into a world-action model, addressing the fact that vision-only futures do not capture force, deformation, shear, or slip in contact-rich manipulation. The method uses a SAF tactile encoder to map tactile appearance, dense force fields, and deformation flow into a shared latent space, adds a tactile-history encoder for temporal context during contact phases, and applies Anchor-Guided Tri-Modal Attention to keep future tactile supervision from leaking directly into the action branch. Experiments are reported on four real-world contact-rich tasks spanning fragile grasping, sustained surface contact, and dynamic in-hand manipulation. The model reaches a 75.0% average success rate, 37.5 percentage points above the strongest baseline, and ablations consistently degrade performance when tactile history is removed or future-target access is relaxed. Overall, the paper makes a strong case that future tactile supervision plus deployment-consistent information flow helps dexterous manipulation; however, the public material still leaves the robot-platform scale, data volume, and failure modes only partially characterized.

## 2. FasTac: A Curved Multispectral Vision-Based Tactile Sensor for High-Speed High-Precision 3D Shape and Force Perception

这是一项面向灵巧机器人曲面指尖的触觉传感器工作，核心目标就是提升灵巧操作中的几何感知、力感知和高速接触感知能力。FasTac 的主要工作是为灵巧操作中的曲面指尖设计一套高精度、高速的视觉触觉传感方案，解决现有曲面视觉触觉传感器在 3D 重建、三轴力估计和实时处理上难以兼顾的问题。方法上，它结合多光谱 photometric stereo、边界先验的快速 Poisson 深度重建，以及面向曲面弹性体非均匀力学响应的 position-aware dynamic convolution 力估计网络，并把整条图像到 $F_z$ 的流水线部署到 FPGA 上。实验报告显示，加入 NIR 照明和边界先验后深度 MAE 从 0.2730 mm 降到 0.0415 mm，正常力与剪切力的 NMAE 分别为 2.74% 和 2.39%，FPGA 延迟也从 3.26 ms 降到 1.09 ms。多物体重建、反馈抓取和振动测量验证了其几何感知、稳定力反馈与动态接触感知能力。总体上，它补强了灵巧手感知基础设施，但公开信息主要停留在传感器与台架实验层面，尚不足以判断其在复杂手部整机系统中的全面表现。

FasTac’s main contribution is a high-precision, high-speed vision-based tactile fingertip designed for curved surfaces in dexterous manipulation, addressing the difficulty existing curved tactile sensors have in jointly delivering accurate 3D reconstruction, three-axis force estimation, and real-time processing. The method combines multispectral photometric stereo, boundary-prior fast Poisson depth reconstruction, and a position-aware dynamic-convolution force network tailored to the nonuniform mechanics of curved elastomers, then deploys the full image-to-$F_z$ pipeline on an FPGA. The experiments report that adding NIR illumination and the boundary prior reduces depth MAE from 0.2730 mm to 0.0415 mm, that the force estimator reaches 2.74% and 2.39% NMAE for normal and shear forces, and that FPGA deployment cuts latency from 3.26 ms on GPU to 1.09 ms. Multi-object reconstruction, feedback grasping, and vibration measurement validate the sensor’s geometric perception, stable force feedback, and dynamic contact sensing. Overall, it is a strong sensing-side contribution for dexterous hands, but the public information is still mostly sensor- and bench-level, so its behavior inside a full hand system remains only partially established.
