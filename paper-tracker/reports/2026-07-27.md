## 条目

### 1. Towards Human-level Dexterous Teleoperation

**日期**：2026-07-13  
**作者/机构**：Puhao Li, Zeyuan Chen, Yingying Wu, Pengkun Wei, Yuyang Li, Tianyu Wang, Jiaxiao Shi, Mingrui Yu, Baoxiong Jia, Song-chun Zhu, Tengyu Liu, Siyuan Huang  
**类型**：paper  
**相关性**：这项工作把灵巧手的手-物协同遥操作作为核心问题，直接面向对象重定向、长时程工具使用和连续手内接触切换，符合本追踪器对“灵巧操作必须居中”的要求。  
**Novelty**：提出 TeleDexter，把人类参考动作转成连续的协同跟踪子目标，并在真实机器人上零样本迁移，且报告了七项灵巧遥操作任务的结果。  
**链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11481

**analysis_zh**：这篇工作的主要工作是提出 TeleDexter，用手-物协同跟踪来推进接近人类水平的灵巧遥操作；它要解决的是现有遥操作系统难以在单手内完成工具交换、重抓取和连续重定位等复杂接触切换的问题。方法上，作者从人类参考动作构造连续协同子目标，用混合奖励把稀疏子目标和稠密轨迹跟踪结合起来，并用单阶段强化学习训练控制器，再结合随机动作遮蔽与域随机化实现零样本迁移到真实机器人。实验覆盖七个挑战性灵巧遥操作任务，横跨两种灵巧手形态，结果显示平均成功率达到 75%，而基线方法基本失败；同时，采集到的示教还能用于行为克隆训练自主策略。整体结论是该方法在复杂手内操作上迈出了明确一步，但公开信息仍不足以判断其对更多手型、更多物体类别和更长期任务的泛化边界。  
**analysis_en**：The main contribution is TeleDexter, a hand-object co-tracking controller aimed at pushing dexterous teleoperation toward human-level in-hand skill. It addresses the gap between today’s teleoperation systems and the ability to swap grasps, reposition objects, and sustain contact transitions within a single hand. The method builds consecutive co-tracking subgoals from human reference motions, trains with a hybrid reward that combines sparse subgoal objectives and dense tracking signals, and uses single-stage RL with random action masking and domain randomization for zero-shot transfer to a real robot. Experiments cover seven challenging dexterous teleoperation tasks across two dexterous hand embodiments, and the paper reports a 75% average success rate where the baselines fail consistently; the collected demonstrations also train autonomous policies via behavior cloning. Overall, the work is a meaningful step toward human-level dexterous teleoperation, but the public information is still insufficient to judge how broadly it generalizes across more hand morphologies, object sets, and longer-horizon tasks.

### 2. Current as Touch: Proprioceptive Contact Feedback for Compliant Dexterous Manipulation

**日期**：2026-07-03  
**作者/机构**：Chenyang Ma, Yunchao Yao, Zhenyu Wei, Ruogu Li, Daniel Szafir, Mingyu Ding / University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill  
**类型**：paper  
**相关性**：这项工作把电机电流与关节状态转化为接触反馈信号，直接服务于顺应性灵巧操作、遥操作和多种灵巧手上的接触丰富任务，属于触觉/本体感觉驱动灵巧操作的核心方向。  
**Novelty**：它不依赖外置触觉或力传感器，而是用 proprioception 学习触觉式接触反馈，并把输出形式设计成适合标准 PD 控制的 compliance reference position。  
**链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03529, https://cat.chenyangma.com/

**analysis_zh**：这篇工作的主要工作是提出一种基于本体感觉的接触反馈框架，用电机电流和关节状态学习“类触觉”信号，从而为顺应性灵巧操作提供控制依据；它要解决的是外置触觉/力传感器成本高、易损且不易部署在低成本灵巧手上的问题。方法上，作者不去直接估计外力，而是预测一个 compliance reference position，也就是让标准 PD 控制器去跟踪的理想关节目标，使位置误差自然生成合适抓力；电流既作为力的代理，也作为可学习的接触表示。实验在多种灵巧手和接触丰富任务上进行，包括脆弱物体处理、持续表面接触、薄片取物和动态负载适应，结果显示这种方法能带来稳定的柔顺抓取、更安全高效的遥操作，并改善后续策略学习；项目页还报告了泡沫杯堆叠、白板擦拭、单卡抓取和动态瓶持等任务上的明显提升。总体来看，这是把本体感觉直接转成“触觉式”控制信号的实用路线，但公开材料对不同硬件平台的完整泛化、失败案例和极限条件仍给得不够充分。  
**analysis_en**：The main contribution is a proprioception-driven contact-feedback framework that uses motor current and joint states to learn tactile-like signals for compliant dexterous manipulation. It addresses the practical problem that external tactile or force sensors are costly, fragile, and hard to deploy on low-cost robot hands. Rather than estimating external wrench signals directly, the method predicts a compliance reference position, meaning an ideal joint target for a standard PD controller whose position error produces the desired grasping force. This makes the approach compatible with mainstream teleoperation and policy-learning pipelines while still adapting interaction forces online. The experiments span multiple dexterous hands and contact-rich tasks, including fragile object handling, sustained surface contact, thin-object retrieval, and dynamic load adaptation; the project page also shows improvements on foam-cup stacking, whiteboard wiping, single-card picking, and dynamic bottle holding. The reported results indicate stable compliant grasping, safer and more efficient teleoperation, and better downstream policy learning without external tactile sensors. Overall, the work is a strong practical direction for proprioceptive dexterity, but the public information is still insufficient to fully assess cross-platform generalization, failure modes, and the hardest edge cases.

### 3. A Monolithic Hand with Asymmetric Origami Bending and Dual-chamber Actuators

**日期**：2026-07-24  
**作者/机构**：Nan Huang, Yuming Zhu, Zicong Zhang, Jianhui Liu, Xiaohuang Liu, Dihan Liu, Jiansheng Dai, Sicong Liu  
**类型**：paper  
**相关性**：这是一项直接面向灵巧手本体设计的工作，核心贡献是单体式软体灵巧手结构、手指-掌部协同动作与更易制造的机械实现，而不是泛化的平行夹爪抓取。  
**Novelty**：提出 asymmetric origami bending 和 asymmetric dual-chamber 设计，并将其整合进 OSOR 手，实现单材料、SLS 打印的一体化制造。  
**链接**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22320

**analysis_zh**：这篇工作的主要工作是提出 Origami-inspired SOft Robotic（OSOR）手，通过 asymmetric origami bending 和 asymmetric dual-chamber 两类结构实现单体式灵巧手；它要解决的是现有软体灵巧手往往结构复杂、制造困难，同时难以兼顾输出性能和被动顺应性的问题。方法上，作者为手指和掌部分别设计 AOB-S 与 AOB-D 单元，建立解析模型并结合有限元分析指导结构设计，再用单一 TPU 材料通过选择性激光烧结完成一体化制造。实验验证了模型与仿真，并报告手指最大弯曲 203°、掌部 40° 的运动范围，以及 6.3 N 和 16 N 的输出力；手还能够完成纸巾夹取、水瓶抓握、仅用掌部抓取以及制造业抓握分类中的多种 power grasps。整体结论是该方案在紧凑性、性能和易制造性之间取得了不错平衡，但公开资料仍缺少更系统的灵巧操作基准、耐久性测试以及与更多主流灵巧手的直接对比。  
**analysis_en**：The main contribution is the OSOR hand, a monolithic soft robotic hand built around asymmetric origami bending and asymmetric dual-chamber actuators. It addresses a classic hardware tradeoff in soft dexterous hands: many designs are mechanically complex, hard to manufacture, and still struggle to balance output performance with passive compliance. The method defines AOB-S and AOB-D units for the fingers and palm, derives analytical models, and uses finite element analysis to guide the geometry before fabricating the hand via selective laser sintering in a single TPU material. The experiments validate the model and simulation, and the paper reports a 203° finger bending range, 40° palm bending, and output forces of 6.3 N and 16 N. Demonstrations include tissue pinching, stable two-finger bottle grasping, palm-only grasping, and several power grasps from manufacturing taxonomy. Overall, the work shows a compact and manufacturable soft dexterous hand with promising motion and force capability, but the public information still lacks a larger dexterous manipulation benchmark, durability testing, and head-to-head comparisons with more established hands.
