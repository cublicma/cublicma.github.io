## 主要条目

### 1. Aero Hand Open

**中文**

Aero Hand Open 的主要工作是公开一套面向灵巧手研究的可复用平台，解决的是多指灵巧手在控制、集成和复现实验上长期缺少统一开源基座的问题。其方法重点不在单一算法，而在把硬件平台、软件接口、控制流程和实验材料打包成可直接使用的研究入口，便于后续在真实手上做灵巧操作、手内操作和学习控制。公开信息显示它强调工程可用性和研究复现，适合承接后续数据采集与算法验证。实验层面，当前公开材料更偏平台展示与系统验证，缺少足够完整的跨任务定量比较，因此对性能上限和泛化能力的结论仍有限。总体上，这是一项偏基础设施的贡献，价值在于降低灵巧手研究门槛，但其限制是公开摘要里尚未给出足够细的基准、失败案例和长期稳定性数据。

**English**

Aero Hand Open mainly contributes an open, reusable platform for dexterous-hand research, addressing the long-standing lack of a unified open-source base for multi-finger hand control, integration, and reproducible experiments. The method is not a single algorithm; instead, it packages hardware, software interfaces, control workflows, and experimental materials into a ready-to-use research entry point for real-hand dexterous manipulation, in-hand manipulation, and learning-based control. The public information emphasizes engineering usability and reproducibility, which makes it a practical foundation for future data collection and algorithm validation. On the experimental side, the available material appears to focus more on platform demonstration and system validation than on a complete cross-task quantitative comparison, so conclusions about performance ceilings and generalization remain limited. Overall, this is an infrastructure contribution whose value lies in lowering the barrier to dexterous-hand research, but the public release still lacks detailed benchmarks, failure analysis, and long-term stability data.

### 2. RoboTok: Internet-scale retrieval engine for human hand trajectories with video prompting to advance dexterous robot manipulation

**中文**

RoboTok 的主要工作是构建一个面向灵巧机器人操作的数据检索引擎，把互联网规模的人类手轨迹和参考视频提示结合起来，为策略学习提供更丰富、可扩展的示范来源。它解决的是灵巧操作领域高质量示教稀缺、人工标注成本高、单一演示覆盖不足的问题。方法上，系统利用手轨迹检索来筛选与任务相关的片段，再通过视频提示组织出可用于学习的参考信号，使数据获取从“少量人工采集”转向“规模化检索与筛选”。公开信息表明作者强调该引擎对灵巧操作学习的增益，但当前可见材料对具体模型结构、训练细节和下游任务设置披露有限。实验部分若以公开摘要为准，主要展示该检索式数据引擎的可行性与对灵巧策略学习的帮助，但缺少足够完整的跨基线数值和真实机器人对照。总体结论是它提供了一条很有潜力的数据扩展路径，限制在于公共信息还不足以判断其在不同手型、不同场景上的稳定收益。

**English**

RoboTok’s main contribution is a retrieval engine for dexterous robot manipulation that combines internet-scale human hand-trajectory retrieval with reference-video prompting to supply richer and more scalable demonstrations for policy learning. It tackles a core bottleneck in dexterous manipulation: scarce high-quality demonstrations, expensive manual annotation, and insufficient coverage from a handful of teleoperated examples. Methodologically, the system filters task-relevant segments through hand-trajectory retrieval and organizes them into learning signals via video prompting, shifting data acquisition from small-scale manual collection toward large-scale retrieval and selection. The public information suggests that the authors position this engine as a way to improve dexterous policy learning, but the available material is light on model architecture, training details, and downstream task specification. The experiments, at least from the public abstract-level information, appear to demonstrate feasibility and utility for dexterous policy learning, yet the release does not disclose enough cross-baseline numbers or real-robot comparisons. Overall, it offers a promising path for scaling data in dexterous manipulation, while the main limitation is that the public information is still insufficient to judge robustness across hand embodiments and scenarios.
