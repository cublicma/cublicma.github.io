## 条目

### InDex: Empowering VLA Models with Intent-Conditioned Arm-Hand Coordination
这项工作的主要工作是将 `InDex` 迭代到更新版本，继续围绕手臂-手部协调与意图条件化适配来增强 VLA 模型在灵巧操作中的可用性；它要解决的是通用 VLA 模型在灵巧手控制上容易出现的形态错配与动作表达不足问题。方法上，公开信息显示作者强调 intent-conditioned 的协调建模，并把 arm-hand 作为统一控制对象来做适配与优化。实验部分说明该版本包含机器人相关验证，但公开资料并未充分披露完整实验设置、基线与全部定量结果，因此无法过度推断。总体结论是，这次更新让 `InDex` 在灵巧手方向更像一个可落地的适配框架，而不是单纯概念性方法；限制在于当前公开信息仍不足以完全判断其泛化范围、失败案例和与更广泛具身平台的兼容性。

The main contribution here is an updated `InDex` release that continues to target intent-conditioned arm-hand coordination for dexterous manipulation, rather than introducing a separate new line of work. It addresses the recurring problem that general-purpose VLA models often struggle with morphology mismatch and insufficient action expressiveness for dexterous hands. The public material indicates an intent-conditioned coordination formulation that treats arm and hand as a unified control object for adaptation and optimization. The experiments appear to include robot validation, but the available public information does not fully disclose the setup, baselines, or all quantitative results, so it would be unsafe to overstate the evidence. The overall conclusion is that this update makes `InDex` look more like a practical adaptation framework for dexterous manipulation, while the limitations remain that the public record is still too thin to judge generalization, failure modes, or compatibility across broader embodied platforms.
